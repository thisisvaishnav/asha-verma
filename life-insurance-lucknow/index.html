Life insurance in Lucknow

Life Insurance Guidance in Lucknow

Life insurance pays money to your nominee if you die during the policy term. It can support your family's financial plans. This page explains the main types of cover. It also explains the points you must check before you buy.

What life insurance does

You pay a premium to the insurer. The insurer pays the cover amount to your nominee if you die during the policy term.

Some plans also pay a maturity amount if you live until the end of the term. Check your plan to see which type you hold.

Life insurance is different from health insurance. Health insurance pays medical costs. Life insurance pays your nominee.

Types of life insurance

Each type has a different purpose. Compare the purpose with your own need before you compare premiums.

  • Term plan. It pays a cover amount if you die during the term. It does not pay a maturity amount.
  • Endowment plan. It pays a cover amount during the term. It also pays a maturity amount if you survive the term.
  • Money-back plan. It pays part of the sum assured at set intervals. It pays the rest at maturity.
  • Unit-linked plan (ULIP). It combines life cover with market-linked funds. Your money can go up or down. Charges apply.
  • Pension plan. It pays a regular income after a set age. Check the options for lump sum and income.

Bonuses, if any, depend on the policy and the performance of the insurer. They are not guaranteed.

Points to check before you buy

  1. The cover amount, and whether it meets your family's needs.
  2. The nominee details, and how to update them later.
  3. The policy term and the premium paying term.
  4. Whether the premium stays the same, or changes with age.
  5. The conditions and exclusions in the policy wording.
  6. The surrender value, if you stop paying before the term ends.
  7. The documents your nominee must submit to make a claim.
Policy benefits, eligibility, exclusions, limits, and conditions vary by product. Review the official policy documents before you buy.

How the money is paid

Your nominee informs the insurer after your death. The nominee submits the policy document and the records that the insurer asks for.

The insurer checks the claim against the policy terms. The money is paid only as the policy describes.

The death benefit can be paid as one lump sum or in installments. Your policy document states which option applies.

Tell your nominee where you keep the policy document. A clear record makes the claim process simpler.

Life insurance for families

Life cover can support your family when your income stops. The right amount depends on your situation.

How to estimate the cover

Add up your income, your liabilities, and the years your family depends on you. Compare that result with the premium you can pay over the full term.
