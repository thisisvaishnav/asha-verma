Privacy Policy

This page explains how your details are handled. It also explains how you can stop further contact.

What this website does

This website gives general information about health and life insurance. It does not sell policies online.

What this website collects

This website has no account system. It does not save your consultation form. This website keeps no copy of your enquiry.

When you press "Send my enquiry", your details are sent to Asha by email. The form service that carries the message is Formspree. You can then also send the same message on WhatsApp.

Details you send to Asha

The form asks for the details below.

  • Your name.
  • Your mobile number.
  • Your preferred contact method.
  • Your insurance requirement: health, life, or both.
  • An optional message from you.
  • Your consent to be contacted.

Do not send Aadhaar numbers, bank details, medical records, or passwords. This form does not ask for them.

How the details are used

Asha uses your details only to answer your enquiry. Asha does not sell your details to anyone.

Services that carry your message

WhatsApp is run by Meta. Formspree carries the form to Asha's email inbox. Your phone network handles your calls. Their privacy policies apply after your message leaves this website.

Cookies and tracking tools

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. It loads a web font from Google Fonts. That service can record a standard server log, which can include your IP address.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites. Their privacy policies apply on their own websites.

Consent

Tick the consent box before you send an enquiry. You then give permission for Asha to contact you about that enquiry.

Stop further contact

Send a WhatsApp message that says "Do not contact me", or call the phone number. Asha will stop contacting you.

Ask to view or correct your details

Call Asha or send a WhatsApp message. Asha will show the details Asha holds, and correct them when you ask.

Children

This website does not offer services to children.
