Health Insurance Guidance in Lucknow

Health insurance helps you pay for medical costs. This page explains what a policy can cover. It also explains the limits you must check. Read this before you choose a plan.

What health insurance does

You pay a premium to the insurer. The insurer pays the eligible cost of treatment, up to the limits in your policy.

You can buy cover for yourself, for your family, or for both. The policy document lists every person who is covered.

Health insurance does not pay every medical bill. It pays only the costs that your policy lists as covered costs.

What a policy can cover

Each policy is different. Most health policies cover some of the points below.

  • Hospital room, nursing, and intensive care costs.
  • Doctor and surgeon fees during a hospital stay.
  • Day-care procedures that do not need an overnight stay.
  • Diagnostic tests before and after hospital care.
  • Ambulance charges, in some policies.
  • Pre-existing conditions after a waiting period, in some policies.

Costs your policy may not cover

  • Medical costs above your sum insured.
  • Treatment that starts during a waiting period.
  • Conditions listed as exclusions in the policy document.
  • Costs that the insurer does not accept as reasonable or required.
  • Cosmetic treatment, in most policies.

Read the policy wording for the exact list. The list changes from one product to another.

Key words explained

Insurance documents use specific words. These short meanings help you read them.

Sum insured

The maximum amount the insurer will pay in a policy year, as set in your policy.

Waiting period

A set time before some covers start. Pre-existing conditions often have a waiting period.

Exclusion

A condition or treatment that the policy does not cover.

Deductible

The amount you pay before the insurer starts to pay.

Co-payment

A share of the bill that you pay. The insurer pays the rest.

Cashless claim

The insurer settles the bill with the hospital. You pay only your own share.

Points to check before you buy

Use this list when you compare two or more policies.

  1. The sum insured, and every person it covers.
  2. Room rent limits and other sub-limits in the policy.
  3. Waiting periods for pre-existing conditions and for specific treatments.
  4. Exclusions listed in the policy wording.
  5. The premium now, and how it can change at renewal.
  6. How to make a claim, and which hospitals are in the network.
  7. The policy term, and the age up to which cover continues.
Policy benefits, eligibility, exclusions, limits, and conditions vary by product. Review the official policy documents before you buy.

Cover for different needs

Health insurance for working professionals

Your employer may offer group health cover. Check the sum insured and the limits. This cover usually stops when you leave the job. A personal policy is not linked to your job.

Family health insurance in Lucknow

You can buy one policy that covers several family members. The sum insured is shared by all members. Some families buy a separate policy for each person. Check how the cover works before you decide.
