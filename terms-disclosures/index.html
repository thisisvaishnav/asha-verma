General information only

This website gives general information about insurance. It is not an offer of insurance. It is not a contract.

No promise that a policy will be issued

Contacting Asha does not mean that a policy will be issued. The insurer decides whether to accept an application.

No promise that a claim will be approved

The insurer decides every claim. A claim must meet the terms of the policy to be paid.

Benefits and limits vary

Benefits, eligibility, exclusions, limits, and conditions vary by insurer and by product. Read the official policy documents before you buy.

No guaranteed returns

Savings plans and market-linked plans can go up or down. No plan on this website promises a fixed return or a fixed payout.

Professional details

Registration and authorisation details appear only after they are verified. Qualifications, insurer affiliations, and languages are listed only when they are confirmed.

Accuracy of information

We work to keep the information correct. Insurance products and terms change. Confirm the current details with the insurer before you decide.

Consultation form

The consultation form sends your enquiry to Asha through Formspree, a form service. This website does not store your enquiry. Sending the form does not mean that you have bought a policy.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp and to other websites are provided for your convenience. Formspree carries the form to Asha's email inbox. Those services apply their own terms.

Your decision

Check that a policy suits your needs, your budget, and your circumstances before you buy it. Ask questions if any point is unclear.

Limitation of liability

To the extent that the law allows, Asha is not responsible for decisions that you make based on general information on this website.

Governing law
